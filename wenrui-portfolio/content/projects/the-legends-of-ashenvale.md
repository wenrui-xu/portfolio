## Overview

The Legends of Ashenvale is a PC game project developed in Unity during a university term by a four-person team. The playable prototype brings together combat, character selection, a shop and progression systems.

## My role

I implemented the main and pause menus, in-game UI, shop and purchase system, experience and progression, combat and attack systems, weapon behaviour, and combat hero selection. I also integrated systems in Unity and worked on debugging and final gameplay polish.

## Challenges

A key challenge was making each weapon behave differently as originally planned while allowing players to upgrade weapons through the progression system. At the same time, our four-person team worked on the Unity project through GitHub from different devices, so we had to coordinate commits and resolve synchronization and integration issues. I debugged the combat and UI systems and helped bring everything together into a polished playable prototype within the academic term.

## Process

My work covered the UI flow, gameplay systems and their integration in Unity, along with debugging and final gameplay polish.

## Outcome

The team completed a polished, playable university prototype within the academic term. [Watch the trailer ↗](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JyPZqnFRGDM).
