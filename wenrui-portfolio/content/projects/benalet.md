## Overview

Benalet is a fair-stall game created for Meta Quest 3 by a two-person team. Players have 60 seconds to knock down moving rubber-duck targets and score points through different physical VR interactions. The prototype brings together controller movement, teleportation, grabbing, a bow, a pistol and configurable difficulty.

## My role

I set up and assembled the playable scene. I implemented voice commands to start or restart a round, recover arrows and change difficulty. I also built the bow-and-arrow interaction, from placing an arrow and drawing the string to firing; the pistol interaction and shooting behaviour; and the duck targets and their gameplay behaviour. I worked with VR locomotion, teleportation, grabbing, scoring and difficulty settings as part of the team.

## Challenges

The bow had to make separate actions—placing an arrow, drawing the string and firing—work as a single VR interaction. The same scene also needed to respond to spoken commands and support different ways of hitting moving targets. I integrated and tested these interactions within the playable round, with the second team member contributing to the shared project.

## Outcome

The two-person team produced a playable VR fair-stall prototype for Meta Quest 3. A public demo and source link can be added when available.
