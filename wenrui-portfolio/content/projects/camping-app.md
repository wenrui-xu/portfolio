## Overview

Camping App is an Android project built by a two-person team. It brings camping information from the Generalitat into a mobile interface with cards and a map. It is a separate application from Animalpedia, although both live in the same university repository.

## My role

I connected the Generalitat data API, designed and implemented the card-based interface, and linked the camping information to the map view. The rest of the app was developed collaboratively by our two-person team.

## Challenges

The central integration challenge was making external camping records useful in two views: compact cards for browsing and map locations for geographic context. I worked on the data connection and presentation of those records across both parts of the interface.

## Outcome

The team completed a working university app. [Watch the Camping App demo ↗](https://youtu.be/fOe8LMVvm80).
