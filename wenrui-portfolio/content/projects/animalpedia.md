## Overview

Animalpedia is a native Android animal guide developed by a two-person team for university coursework. It has its own functionality and case study, even though its source is stored in the same course repository as Camping App.

## My role

I handled project documentation and organisation, implemented Daynimal (the animal of the day, calculated from the date), connected the Pixabay API for imagery, and worked on local user management with Room. I also resolved bugs and collaborated on the GUI design.

## Challenges

Daynimal needed a predictable selection tied to the date. I implemented the date-based calculation and integrated it with the app's animal content while working with my teammate on the wider Android project. I also connected external images and local user data without presenting those shared features as my solo work.

## Outcome

The team completed the university app. [Watch the Animalpedia demo ↗](https://youtu.be/tgQoQplQvf0). The shared course repository also contains Camping App, a separate project.
