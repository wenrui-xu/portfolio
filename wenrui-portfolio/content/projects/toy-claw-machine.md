## Overview

Toy Claw Machine is a small solo Unity prototype based on the familiar arcade claw-machine interaction. It is presented as a focused gameplay study rather than a released game.

## My role

I developed the interactive prototype in Unity and prepared the short demonstration. The scope centres on making one arcade interaction playable and easy to understand.
