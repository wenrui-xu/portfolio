## Overview

This solo Unity project uses a controllable helicopter to explore a prepared interior scene. The first- and third-person views make the helicopter the central interaction while the environment demonstrates baked lighting, Light Probes and material effects.

## My role

I developed the helicopter controls and views, arranged traversal through the scene, set up baked lighting and Light Probes, and created shader-based materials for the vases and a custom noise shader for a television screen.

## Outcome

The resulting interactive scene combines control, environment lighting and shader work in one demo. [Watch from the helicopter segment ↗](https://youtu.be/wunzRa6l6bU?t=261).
